## [BREATHE](https://www.climatebristol.org/projects/breathe/)

BREATHE (Bridging Research on Environmental Attribution and Health Equity) is an ambitious international consortium, coming together to advance our knowledge of climate and health attribution. 

This Wellcome trust funded project brings together researchers from the University of Bristol (UK), University of Oxford (UK), London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine (UK), Office for National Statistics (UK), CIDACS (Brazil), FFU (Brazil), PIK (Germany), Estación Biológica de Doñana - CSIC (Spain), and the University of Cape Town (South Africa), along with a wide range of international partners and stakeholders. 

My role within the project is the development and analysis of a new detection and attribution large ensemble from the University of Bristol's HadCM3B-ESM climate model. This large ensemble - which will feature 200+ initialisations, multiple experiments, and both concentration- and emission-driven simulations - will be based on the DAMIP2 protocol and submitted to CMIP7. With our large ensemble we aim to investigate the contribution of anthropogenic emissions to climate-related health outcomes, particularly in the UK and Brazil.
